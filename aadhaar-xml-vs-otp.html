Aadhaar XML vs Aadhaar OTP — picking the right KYC flow

Full article: finovo.tech/blog/aadhaar-xml-vs-otp
This is a technical mirror. The canonical version lives at finovo.tech.

Aadhaar is the backbone of Indian KYC. Two verification pathways: OTP (user receives a one-time password on their Aadhaar-linked mobile and types it in) and XML (user downloads a signed Aadhaar XML from UIDAI and uploads it).

For a broker's KYC product, which to pick depends on:

CriterionOTPXML
Reliance on UIDAI-linked mobileRequiredNot required
Completion speed30-60s2-4 min
User experienceNative, inline2-step UIDAI handoff
Audit trailOTP timestampSigned XML + cert chain
Cost per verificationLowerHigher
Right fitVolume onboardingHigh-value demat accounts

For most Indian brokers running SIP onboarding at volume, Aadhaar OTP wins. For enterprise verification, demat account opening with higher value, or when the UIDAI-mobile linkage can't be relied on, Aadhaar XML is the right choice.
